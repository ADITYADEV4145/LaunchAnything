Small teams lose more time than they think just finding out what this week is about. I have watched my own team do it in chat, in docs and in meetings that only exist to answer that question.

I built Teamnest as the smallest test of an answer. The landing page introduces a shared weekly board and links to pricing. There is no hosted product behind it yet.

I am trying to learn two things before I write more code. Would a team check one board every Monday? And would they pay for it, or is a shared document good enough?

If you run a small team, I would like to hear how you plan the week today.
