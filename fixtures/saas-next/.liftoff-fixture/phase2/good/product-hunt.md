Planning a small team week gets messy when the plan lives in five different places. The landing page introduces a shared weekly board and links to pricing.

## Maker comment

I made Teamnest because my own team kept losing the plan for the week between chat threads and documents. This repository is the landing page for that idea: it introduces a shared weekly board and links to pricing. It is a demo, not a hosted service yet, and there are no customer results to share. I want to learn whether a single weekly board is enough for a small team. What would your team need to see on Monday morning before it trusts a board like this?
